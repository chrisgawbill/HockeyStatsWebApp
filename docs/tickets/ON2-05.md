# ON2-05 — Screens and Clear Crease battles

Delivery rules: [development workflow](../agent-development.md).

**Product Owner checkpoint:** Net-front eligibility, screen expiry on turnovers, and initial save penalty.

**Status:** IDEA

**Net-front cards (approved by Product Owner in ON2-04, 2026-10-28):**

Starting values, tuned in playtest. The screener holding the net front plays
the possession side; the defenseman clearing the crease plays the challenger
side. Values assume the screener holds the spot on a timeout; confirm at
kickoff. "Breaks resistance" is the effect added in ON2-04.

| Card | Side | Cost | Effect | Tag | Deck placement |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Establish Position | Screener | 1 | 5 resistance | block | Universal core |
| Find the Rebound | Screener | 1 | 3 pressure, draw 1 | skill | Universal core |
| Hold Your Ground | Screener | 2 | 11 resistance | block | Heavy Game, replacing its Protect Puck |
| Clear the Crease | Defenseman | 2 | 10 pressure | check | Universal core |
| Lift the Stick | Defenseman | 1 | 3 pressure, breaks resistance | check | Universal core |
| Front the Shooter | Defenseman | 1 | 3 pressure, 3 resistance | block | Defensive Structure, replacing its Read the Lane |

Existing cards that also gain the net-front context: Brace for Contact
(screener) and Body Check (defenseman). Box Out is not added; it overlaps
Clear the Crease. The universal core grows from 21 to 25 cards.

**Goal:** Add one meaningful off-puck setup and its symmetric defensive counter.

**Read:** design “Off-puck play and screens”; rink zones, actions, duel/card
contracts, shot-save model, reducer, and focused tests.

**Dependencies:** ON2-02, ON2-03, and approved net-front cards from ON2-04.

**Scope:**

- Define net-front eligibility and screen state.
- Let an eligible forward spend its action to Set Screen.
- Let an eligible defenseman spend its action to Clear Crease through a card
  battle.
- Remove screens on movement, successful clear, zone-possession exit, or stoppage.
- Apply at most one small visible save-chance modifier.
- Give CPU the same setup/clear capabilities with a minimal legal heuristic.

**Acceptance:**

- Both sides set and clear screens under identical rules.
- A screen costs an activation and cannot stack.
- Every removal/reset condition is tested.
- Shot explanation shows the exact modifier and remains clamped.
- CPU neither ignores every screen nor receives automatic clears.
- No bundle of extra timing/power/rebound/poise modifiers is added.

**Exclusions:** tips, deflections, multiple screens, goalie interference, team
screen bonuses.

**Verify:** action/duel/shot/reducer/CPU tests, full board-game tests, typecheck,
build.
