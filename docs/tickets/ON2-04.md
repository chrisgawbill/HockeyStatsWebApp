# ON2-04 — Hockey card library and identity packages

Delivery rules: [development workflow](../agent-development.md).

**Product Owner checkpoint:** Proposed card list, identity strengths, weaknesses, and functional budgets.

**Status:** ACTIVE — started 2026-10-28; card list and packages approved.

**Decisions (Product Owner, 2026-10-28):**

- Identities follow the API-ON2-02 contract's five systems, replacing the six
  names first listed here: Heavy Forecheck (`forecheck`), Speed & Transition
  (`transition`), Puck Possession (`possession`), Heavy Game (`physical`),
  Defensive Structure (`structure`). Rush Attack and Counterattack merge into
  Speed & Transition; Net-Front Pressure becomes Heavy Game.
- Net-front cards are defined in [ON2-05](ON2-05.md) and ship there, when the
  net-front battle exists.
- Card text uses pressure/resistance; internal effect names stay `damage`/`block`.
- One new effect, "breaks resistance": the opponent's resistance is removed for
  that round before pressure applies. No new shot or faceoff cards.
- All values below are starting points, tuned in playtest.

Budget guide: about 5 points per energy (pressure/resistance 1 point, draw or
breaks resistance about 3), plus about 4 for an exhaust card.

Deck shape (27 cards; ON2-06's signature card makes 28):

- Universal core (21): Deke, Toe Drag, Protect Puck ×2, Stickhandle, Slip the
  Check; Poke Check ×2, Body Check, Angle Out, Stick Lift, Read the Lane;
  Wrist Shot, Snap Shot, Slapshot; Quick Hands, Tie It Up, Forehand Pull,
  Win It Back.
- Primary identity (4 slots): three battle cards plus one shot or faceoff card.
- Secondary identity (2 slots): the package's first two battle cards; must
  differ from the primary.
- Neutral fill when no identity is set: primary Deke, Poke Check, Protect
  Puck, One-Timer; secondary Stickhandle, Poke Check.
- Every assembled deck holds at least 5 eligible cards per ordinary battle and
  side (enforced by test).

| Package | Primary (secondary = first two) | Strength | Weakness |

**Goal:** Expand the playbook into balanced situational hockey actions and define
reusable team-identity packages.

**Read:** design “Hockey meaning” and “Playbook composition”;
card/balance data and effect helpers.

**Dependencies:** ON2-03; Product Owner approves the proposed card list and
package tradeoffs.

**Scope:**

- Add the smallest useful set for puck-carrier, challenger, passing,
  interception, shot, and faceoff decisions (net-front cards: see ON2-05).
- Lead with pressure/resistance/poise language rather than damage/block.
- Define universal core plus the five identity packages listed under Decisions.
- Give packages comparable card counts and functional budgets with stated
  strengths and weaknesses.
- Add only effects supported by current state or explicitly tested here.
- Reserve signature-card templates for ON2-06.

**Acceptance:**

- Every card has valid participant contexts and concise hockey copy.
- Every ordinary context has enough eligible cards for varied offers.
- No card is dominated at the same cost/context without situational reason.
- Every package replaces equivalent slots and declares a tradeoff.
- Deterministic tests cover each effect and package composition.

**Exclusions:** team assignments, bespoke team cards, player ratings, card UI.

**Verify:** card/effect/package tests, full board-game tests, typecheck, build,
Product Owner content review.
